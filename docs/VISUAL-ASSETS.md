# Cart render provenance

The final assets are `cart-precedent-front.webp` and `cart-precedent-rear.webp`. Both were generated using the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and compressed for the app with alpha preserved. These are illustrative model previews, not photographs of the user's installed wiring.

The user approved a 2008 Club Car Precedent, white body and black trim. Earlier polygon and newer Onward-style drafts were discarded. Tan canopy/seating remain as in the approved render.

The final front edit prompt was: preserve the exact approved white 2008 Precedent, camera, wheels, canopy and seats; replace only its small bumper light insert with a NOKINS-style central headlight bank, segmented RGB wings and lower U-shaped accent, using the manufacturer's fixture image as a shape reference; keep LEDs unlit, preserve the classic smooth cowl and black trim, and produce a clean transparent cutout.

The final rear prompt was: render the same white 2008 Precedent from behind, with black trim, tan canopy/seats, rear-facing bench and rear step; show both rear wedge-shaped taillight housings on the rear shoulders, keeping their RGB perimeter areas unlit for the software overlay; preserve the same model proportions and a transparent background.

Shape references were dealer photographs of a 2008 Precedent and NOKINS fixture images. Reference photographs are not distributed in the app. Colored zone strokes, NOKINS RGB accents and windshield LED eyes are separate software-drawn layers; they do not alter the underlying generated vehicle images and do not establish hardware transfer support.
