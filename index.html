🎨 Color calibration: RGB swaps, white, teal & other colors

Send raw RED, GREEN and BLUE commands, then record the actual color on the strip—even if it is white, orange or teal. A simple RGB swap can be corrected automatically. Mixed or unexpected colors require manual matching or a check of the strip/controller configuration. No SP608E settings are changed.

If each raw primary produces a different pure primary, Save RGB swap can automatically fix all mixed colors. If you see white, orange, teal, or something else, record the observation and use the manual mixer below. We will not guess a false RGB mapping.

Uncalibrated: standard RGB commands. Test one output at a time while parked.
