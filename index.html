Connection notes

Close SceneX/BanlanX before connecting: most SP608E units allow one active BLE controller. Bluefy needs this page on HTTPS; Safari does not expose the required API.

After installing, connect once and use Read SP608E before making changes. No login, analytics, or external scripts.
